# The game's patch notes, kept by hand

Azeroth Survivors publishes its patch notes on its Discord; its update server carries only the newest
build's first paragraph. This file holds the full posts so the Satchel can show them (it fetches this
file from the repository every half hour). One section per version, newest first, in the format the
Satchel's own CHANGELOG.md uses: `## <version> (<YYYY-MM-DD>)` (the date is optional), the summary
paragraph, then the detail lines, each starting with `- ` (a line without it goes on with the line
before, as a wrapped line does). Paste the developer's post as it is; drop the trailer line and any
user mentions. The text is the game developer's; this file only mirrors it for players of the game.

## 0.141.0 (2026-11-04)

Scholomance's bosses now drop more often, one piece at a time, and give you what you are missing first. Kill Command and Heart of the Packlord's damage now shows under each pet on the meter, and Tranquility counts all of your healing again.

- Scholomance loot: each of a boss's two pieces has a 25% chance (it was 12.5%), so a kill gives you a piece half the time. You never get two from one kill, and while you are missing one of the two, a drop is always the one you are missing. Once you own both, either can drop again. The Armory shows the chance you really have.
- Darkmaster Gandling is unchanged: a 25% chance per kill for your class's weapons, and a class with two gets both together.
- Tranquility: its strike now counts all of the healing from your heals again, overhealing included, even at full health or with Tree of Life's shield already full. Tree of Life's shield keeps its cap.
- Heart of the Packlord and Kill Command no longer have their own damage meter lines. Each pet's strikes, the Raptor's bleed, the Matriarch's shockwave and the extra damage from the Wolf's howl now count under that pet. Your total damage is the same.
- Soulfire: extra target upgrades now work past three targets, and its tooltip shows the real number.
- Counterattack: now sets off a free Immolation Trap instead of the removed Explosive Trap, with the same 15% and 30% chance and your trap upgrades.
- Resonant Shocks: your Shocks now strike 1 extra nearby enemy at every rank, for 10%, 20% or 30% of their damage.
- Improved Mind Blast: the tooltip now shows the real total at each rank, from 6% to 30%. The spell itself has not changed.
- Ras Frostwhisper: while his shield is up he takes 75% less damage (it was 45%), and its two runes now stand as towers like Sapphiron's, rising as the shield runs out. Touching one still breaks the shield and leaves him weakened.
- Damage meter: the list now scrolls, so no line is cut off at the bottom of the window, including after a Reset.


## 0.140.0

A new zone opens after Blackrock Mountain: Scholomance, fought in Rattlegore's Great Ossuary. Five new bosses bring twenty new mechanics with exact warnings on the ground, and they drop the Tier 0.5 sets and a weapon for every class.

- New zone: Scholomance, between Blackrock Mountain and the Gates of Ahn'Qiraj. Anyone who has already passed that point keeps everything they have opened.
- Five bosses, at 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20 minutes: Kirtonos the Herald, Ras Frostwhisper, Doctor Theolen Krastinov, Lord Alexei Barov and Darkmaster Gandling. If one still stands when the next is due, the next waits for it.
- Kirtonos changes form at 65% health, then alternates on a set rhythm below 30%. Each form has its own attacks.
- Ras strikes half the floor at a time with a checkerboard, and at 60% health armors himself: touch one of his two runes to break the armor and make him vulnerable.
- Krastinov dashes, cleaves a half circle to one side and then the other, spins through the room in Butcher's Bladestorm as he walks after a distant hero, and hooks whoever stands farthest away.
- Barov strikes in rings rolling outward, each shown just before the last lands, burns the ground, marks an X and only then a +, and sends a moving wall of shadow with a gap to run through.
- Gandling calls you to Headmaster's Office, then the whole floor burns but for one small square in the middle: run for it when he speaks. He also fills the room with four bands and three safe gaps in Class Dismissed, marks whoever stands farthest with Pop Quiz, and at 35% health calls The Last Examination: get to the line, then cross it as one half of the room burns and then the other.
- Four Guardians hold the corners of the room: Risen Construct, Necrofiend, Spectral Tutor and Scholomance Dark Summoner, with two moves each.
- Every class gets its Tier 0.5 set, eight pieces with their own stats and new 4-piece, 8-piece and five-star bonuses: Heroism, Soulforge, Beastmaster (with a permanent Devilsaur Matriarch), Darkmantle, Virtuous, The Five Thunders, Sorcerer's, Deathmist and Feralheart.
- Loot: each of the first four bosses has one 25% roll for one of two armor slots. Gandling has one 25% roll for your class's weapon, and Rogues and Shamans get both of theirs together.
- New achievements: Stormstrike Graduation, Straight A's, You Are Not Prepared! and Yes, I am prepared!: a solo Heroic V clear with each of the nine classes, each in its full Tier 0.5 set at 3 stars and its matching talents. It opens the Lifestealing weapon glow.
- On Heroic II and up, Relentless speeds up these bosses' moves as it does every other boss's, apart from Ras's Frozen Checkmate and Kirtonos's last phase, which keep their rhythm.
- Armor counts for less against these five bosses' mechanics: past 40% damage reduction, only half of the rest applies, so a failed mechanic still costs a well-armored hero.
- Scholomance's new text is translated into every language.
- Sweeping Storm, rank 3: Stormstrike and Windfury now deal 20% more damage, in place of 10% more Stormstrike critical damage. It still adds 2 targets.
- Heroism: Shield Slam no longer strikes again. The 8-piece now gives it 20% more damage and the 5-star 30%, beside its damage from your maximum health.
- Consecration deals 50% more damage for every Paladin. The Amulet of the Inferno no longer adds its own 50% damage; it keeps its area, duration and explosions.
- New Hunter spell: Kill Command replaces Explosive Trap. Each of your pets charges its own target, bosses and elites first: the Wolf bites, the Raptor strikes three times and the Devilsaur Matriarch lands a crushing blow. It is offered once you have a pet. Counterattack still sets off its free Explosive Trap.
- Heart of the Earthshaker: Explosive Trap becomes Heart of the Packlord: the Wolf's howl makes 6 nearby enemies take 15% more from you (at most 20% as the necklace grows), the Raptor's strikes bleed nearby enemies, and the Matriarch's bite sends out a shockwave.
- Giantstalker's 8-piece now makes Kill Command's cooldown 20% shorter and its damage 30% higher.
- Beastmaster: the Devilsaur Matriarch's bites fall from 200% to 150% of the Wolf's, and from double to 50% more against Hunter's Mark targets. The 5-star bonus raises that to 100% more, down from 150%, and her bites no longer open a bleeding wound.
- Wyvern Sting now sets an Immolation Trap at your feet every 5 seconds, not an Explosive Trap.
- Rapid Fire: its free Aimed Shots now come at most twice a second.
- Mind Blast's base damage rises from 130 to 184, and Improved Mind Blast also adds 6% damage per rank.
- The Eye of the Tempest's first line now reads "Stormstrike: +35% damage to bosses and elites".
- Tranquility's strike now counts only healing that restored health or became Tree of Life's shield. Healing that was simply lost no longer fuels it.
- Seed of Life's two hits on a boss or elite now roll their critical strikes separately.
- Critical hits from Tranquility and Seed of Life now trigger Blood Frenzy and The Eye of Hakkar.
- Power Word: Shield now holds at most 100% of its target's maximum health, and never more than 200%. The Eternal Talisman's +26% absorb becomes +50% to that cap, growing with its rank.
- Headmaster's Office: the purple now visibly closes in on the safe square until it strikes.
- Companions with Rune-hound now fetch runes inside your pickup radius too.
- Fixed: pressing Enter to confirm an amulet also opened chat, so WASD typed instead of moving you.
- Rockbiter Weapon's base damage is halved.
- Windfury Weapon, rank 3: a 25% chance of two extra strikes or a 12.5% chance of three.
- Flametongue Weapon, rank 3: the free Fire Nova now deals 50% more damage, up from 30%.
- The Eye of the Tempest now also gives +35% damage to bosses, elites and Guardians.
- The Eternal Talisman, Paladin: Divine Shield lasts twice as long, 12 seconds at the base necklace, with at least 4 seconds between shields.
- The Eternal Talisman, Mage: in place of its absorb bonuses, Ice Barrier's absorb cap doubles to 200% of your max health. A barrier starts smaller, and damage and talents fill the room.
- The White Devilsaur look now shows two white devilsaurs, one for your wolf and one for your raptor, each biting as that pet does.
- The Insane now needs no deaths at all: everyone in a party of three or more must survive 40 minutes of Heroic V Naxxramas without one falling.
- The Whole Armory now counts the Tier 0.5 pieces too, and what you already own counts. Heroic Across Azeroth now says which zones you still have to beat.
- The damage meter shows Windfury's extra strikes under Windfury Weapon for addons too, and gives the Tier 0.5 sets' own damage its own lines.
- Fixed shields growing without limit when something else kept soaking damage between casts, such as Dwarf Stoneform with Soul Link or Fortified. Two friends' shields of the same spell no longer add up: the stronger one holds.
- Darkmantle: Gouge and Kidney Shot no longer hide Marked for Death's icon. The mark itself was never lost.
- Rogues reach at least 8 yards with their melee attacks and auto-attack.
- A run now starts only when you press a movement key, not when your hero settles onto the floor.
- Enemies that stop to fight on a slope no longer stand inside the hill.


## 0.139.2

Addons can now find and mark what is in the world: friends who are down, enemies, caches, guardians, bosses and lairs, in dungeons and raids too.

- New for addons: AZS_GetDownedPartyMembers gives where each downed friend can be brought back, on every map, so an addon can point you to them.
- New for addons: AZS_GetWorldAnchors and AZS_ProjectWorldAnchors put a marker on any enemy, on the spot it fell, or on a cache, guardian, boss or lair, without selecting it or showing its nameplate, and lift it any number of yards above that spot.
- New for addons: combat text and world markers now tell the distance from your hero, so an addon can size text by how far the enemy is whatever the camera does.

## 0.139.1

Fixes the old WoW menu opening behind the pause menu, where a click could hit its Exit Game and close the game. Menus and windows now stop your clicks and scrolling from reaching what's behind them. The Windows version no longer crashes after alt-tabbing when run on Linux through Proton or Wine.

- The Windows version run on Linux (Proton, Wine, Steam, Lutris and the like) no longer crashes after alt-tabbing.
- Escape no longer opens the old WoW game menu behind the pause menu, so clicking Options or the space between the buttons can't press Exit Game.
- Clicks and the mouse wheel on any menu or window stay on it: nothing behind it gets clicked, and the camera no longer zooms while you scroll over the pause menu.
- Moving the HUD or the damage meter no longer clicks what's behind them.
- Enter on the hero picker no longer reaches the character select behind it.
- Binding a key in the options no longer also runs that key's old action.
- Gamepad: Back opens the character pane again.
- Chinese, Japanese and Korean input methods in the downloaded game: finished text reaches the chat box again, and Enter or Space choosing a character no longer also acts on the chat line or your key bindings.


## 0.139.0

Engine update: the game now runs on a newer version of the client it is built on. Everything should play as it did before. Addons can now tell a proc's hit from the spell it borrows.

- Under-the-hood engine improvements from the client the game is built on.
- For addon authors: combat text events now say which damage meter line each hit counts toward, as meterSource (the line's name) and meterSourceKey (its sourceKey in AZS_GetMeterSnapshot). A Shadow Weaving strike that floats as Shadow Word: Pain says Shadow Weaving, and a pet's or a summon's swing says whose it is.
