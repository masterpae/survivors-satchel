What's new in the game · 0.141.0

Scholomance's bosses now drop more often, one piece at a time, and give you what you are missing first. Kill Command and Heart of the Packlord's damage now shows under each pet on the meter, and Tranquility counts all of your healing again.

  • Darkmaster Gandling is unchanged: a 25% chance per kill for your class's weapons, and a class with two gets both together.
  • Soulfire: extra target upgrades now work past three targets, and its tooltip shows the real number.
  • Damage meter: the list now scrolls, so no line is cut off at the bottom of the window, including after a Reset.

What this shows

  • The 0.9 shape: three words on top, the game and Play in a rail on the left, the page beside it. Playtime is a Home card that opens on click; the progress versions are the last section of Settings.
  • Browse's table with the addon pane beside it (400 px; up to 520 px maximised), as plan 0.8 part 2 describes them, inside that shape.
  • Settings that save as you click: each switch says "✓ Saved", and there is no Save button.
  • A first start of one screen: the game found, one Start button.
  • Both themes and three window sizes (1280 × 720, the 1120 × 630 minimum, maximised), flat surfaces without shadows, as the real renderer draws them; "Game running" greys out Play.

Not shown: the cards and list views, the Removed page, the tray, the Play card, real art.

Addons controls: Current is v2's two rows; One row folds them into one slim row with a Filters popover; In the rail is the hybrid: search and the filters in the rail, the sub-pages as a segmented control above the table. The filters really filter the table.

Maximised: the rail stays, the cards stay in two columns, centred, the table and pane grow with the window.

The Browse table and the pane carry the catalog's real versions, dates and downloads. The safety levels, playtimes, progress versions and PCs are sample values.

Principles

One primary action per screen: Play.

Every screen readable in three seconds.

No setting a player has to discover: defaults work, and the first start is one screen.

Details on click, never up front.

The same words everywhere.

Nothing destructive without a question.
